    🪄 About the game

    Hanzi Riddle Box is an AI-powered riddle game for anyone who loves Chinese characters. Here you’ll take on fun character-guessing challenges with an AI host!

    🎮 How to play

    The AI gives you a classic character riddle (like “一箭穿心”, an arrow through the heart). Guess the character from the clue, and the AI will give hints based on your answers before revealing the solution.

    ✨ Features

    • Smart checking: the AI knows whether your answer is right
    • Helpful hints: wrong guesses get a clue, not the answer
    • Endless riddles: a different riddle every time
    • Saved games: your history is saved, so you can return to any game

    👥 Great for

    • Chinese character enthusiasts
    • Chinese language learners
    • Livening up a classroom
    • Casual brain teasers

    Test your knowledge of Chinese characters and enjoy their charm along the way!
